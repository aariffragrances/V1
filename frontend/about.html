    About Aarif Fragrances

    Premium attars & perfumes from Tamil Nadu, India

    Aarif Fragrances is a dedicated perfume and attar brand based in Tamil Nadu, India. Our collection follows eight premium fragrance families: Aquatic Fresh, Fruity Delights, Spicy & Aromatic, Mystic Oud, Floral Elegance, Rich Woody, Sweet Gourmand, and Heritage Traditional.

    All our perfumes are available in convenient sizes — 6ml, 12ml, 30ml, and 50ml — perfect for personal use, gifting, or travel.

    We believe everyone deserves authentic, long-lasting fragrances at fair prices. Order easily on WhatsApp and get delivery across Tamil Nadu and India.
